# Question bank

A well to draw from, not a script. Ask **one at a time**. Reword freely to fit the conversation, and build follow-ups from the user's own words whenever you can; those beat anything here.

Questions marked ★ have historically been the strongest openers. The feedback loop (`SKILL.md`, Step 7) may add, cut or re-mark questions over time.

## Pass 1 — The wide shot

- ★ What's eating most of your week right now?
- ★ If a friend you hadn't seen in a year asked how you are, and actually meant it, what would you tell them?
- What's different about your life now compared to six months ago?
- What are you looking forward to?
- What's the thing you'd do more of if the week had an extra day?

## Pass 2 — Follow-ups by area

Only go into an area the user has opened. One or two levels deep, then move on.

**Work / building**
- What are you building, and who is it for?
- What's the hard part right now?
- What did a good day on it look like recently?
- Is this the main thing, or a side thing that's become the main thing?

**Learning / reading**
- What are you learning that you didn't know at the start of the year?
- What's the best thing you've read lately? What stuck?
- What are you learning *badly*, on purpose?

**Place**
- Where are you based these days? (Accept a city or a country. Never push for more.)
- Has where you live changed how you spend your days?

**Body / health** (only if the user brings it up)
- What are you doing for your body these days?
- Is that a goal thing or a sanity thing?

**People** (only if the user brings it up)
- Who are you spending your time with?
- Is anyone pulling you into something new?

**Making / play**
- What are you making just because you like making it?
- What's the hobby that's quietly taking over?

**Saying no**
- ★ What are you deliberately *not* doing right now?
- Is there anything you'd like people to stop asking you to do?

## Pass 3 — The sweep

One light question per untouched area. Accept "nothing there" happily.

- Anything you're reading or learning at the moment?
- Where are you based these days?
- Anything outside work taking up real time?

## Refresh mode

- Last time you said [line from old page]. Still true?
- What's on your mind now that wasn't on the page at all?
- Anything on the old page you're glad to be done with?

## Closers

- ★ Is there anything you wish I'd asked?
- If the whole page could only be one sentence, what would it be?

## Don't ask

- Anything about relationships, health, money or family that the user hasn't raised first.
- "What are your goals?" It gets résumé answers. Ask what they're doing instead.
- Two questions in one message.
